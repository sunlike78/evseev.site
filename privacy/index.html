Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Atlassian Jira Cloud & Forge Applications

Neural Void builds enterprise applications for the Atlassian Cloud ecosystem using the official Atlassian Forge serverless architecture (including Sprint Scope Guard & Agile JQL Pro, Smart Defaults & DoD Guard, and JSM Outgoing Email Audit).

  • Zero External Data Egress: Our Forge applications execute strictly within Atlassian's secure cloud multi-tenant infrastructure. Your Jira issues, project metadata, user profiles, and story point logs never leave your Atlassian Cloud tenant.
  • No External Servers or Storage: We do not operate external databases, tracking servers, or log aggregators for app issue data. Application states are stored solely in Atlassian Forge Hosted Storage (KVS / App Storage).
  • No Data Resale or Advertising: We do not track, profile, resell, or monetize customer data.
  • GDPR & Security Compliance: Data residency and access control strictly inherit your Atlassian Cloud organization's security and privacy boundaries.

Neural Void Website

This website (neural-void.com) does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or client-side analytics scripts. We do not collect personal data through the site itself.

Cloudflare Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect basic server-side analytics (page views, country, referrer) without setting cookies or collecting personal identifiers. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
